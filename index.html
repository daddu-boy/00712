Direct sunlight at an opening

Section 15 of the Easements Act 1882 gives an absolute right to light and air after twenty years’ peaceable enjoyment, and the suit must be brought within two years of the obstruction. Whether there is interference at all is arithmetic — below.

Mark the neighbour’s building as an obstruction on the Building tab first, or there is nothing to block the light and every figure will come back unchanged.

Source records

Every file is hashed with SHA-256 the moment it is read. Section 63(4) of the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam 2023 requires the certificate accompanying an electronic record to state its hash value and to be signed by both the person in charge of the device and an expert. Provenance cannot be added later, so it is recorded from the first click.
